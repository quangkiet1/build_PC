## 4.3.1. Kich ban 1: Kiem thu thieu ngan sach

Muc tieu: Kiem tra kha nang tinh toan va chan luong truy van cua he thong khi ngan sach nguoi dung dua ra thap hon gia tri thuc te cua linh kien yeu cau.

Dieu kien kiem thu:
- Nguoi dung da co 1 CPU tren ke hang voi gia `12.000.000 VND`.
- Ngan sach con lai truyen vao ham `diChoVaRapThu()` la `-2.000.000 VND`.
- He thong nhan ve 1 GPU de xuat tu kho co gia `9.000.000 VND`.

Ky vong:
- He thong khong them linh kien moi vao ke hang.
- Danh sach linh kien tren ke hang giu nguyen.
- Luong xu ly dung lai truoc khi ghi de du lieu moi.

Ket qua thuc te:
- Test `tests/chatbot-budget.test.ts` da `PASS`.
- `doGoiYTuKho = []`.
- `danhSachTrenKeNganSachMoi` giu nguyen CPU ban dau.

Nhan xet thuc nghiem:
Ket qua cho thay he thong xu ly dung truong hop thieu ngan sach. Nho co che doi chieu gia trong ham `diChoVaRapThu()`, moi linh kien co gia lon hon `nganSachConLai` deu bi bo qua ngay tai vong lap mua hang. Dieu nay giup he thong chan viec ghi de cau hinh bang cac linh kien vuot ngan sach, dong thoi giu nguyen trang thai ke hang hien tai cua nguoi dung.

Lenh kiem thu da chay:

```bash
npm test -- tests/chatbot-budget.test.ts
```
