## 4.3.2. Kich ban 2: Kiem thu xu ly loi nghen mang

Muc tieu: Dam bao kha nang chiu loi cua he thong, giup giao dien nguoi dung khong bi ngat hoac sap trang khi ket noi voi API Google Gemini bi gian doan.

Dieu kien kiem thu:
- Nguoi dung da dang nhap hop le.
- GUI mot truy van chat den `/api/ai/chat`.
- Ham `xuLyTinNhan()` duoc gia lap nem ra loi `Gemini upstream timeout`.

Ky vong:
- API route khong lam sap luong xu ly.
- He thong tra ve phan hoi loi than thien voi ma trang thai `500`.
- He thong khong ghi them ban ghi chat moi vao database trong lan xu ly that bai.
- Giao dien chat co the hien thong bao du phong de nguoi dung tiep tuc su dung trang.

Ket qua thuc te:
- Test `tests/chatbot-network.test.ts` da `PASS`.
- API tra ve ma `500`.
- Truong `error` trong response chua thong diep loi than thien ve he thong AI.
- `prisma.tinNhanChat.createMany()` khong bi goi.

Nhan xet thuc nghiem:
Ket qua cho thay he thong xu ly tot tinh huong nghen mang hoac loi tu dich vu Gemini. Nho khoi `try...catch` tai tang `api routes`, loi phat sinh duoc co lap va chuyen thanh phan hoi loi co kiem soat thay vi lam vo luong xu ly. Giao dien khong bi sap, nguoi dung van nhan duoc thong bao du phong trong khung chat, tu do trai nghiem su dung duoc duy tri lien mach.

Lenh kiem thu da chay:

```bash
npm test -- tests/chatbot-network.test.ts
```
